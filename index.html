A more useful way to think about AI

There is no shortage of AI tools. There is also no shortage of advice about AI. The harder question is usually much simpler: “What should we actually do with it?”

That is where Nadovis Consult comes in.

We start with the organization, not the technology. We look at how work gets done, where time is being lost, where decisions are difficult, where customers are being underserved and where information is sitting unused. From there, we identify the AI opportunities that are worth pursuing.

We work with leaders and teams to turn AI from an interesting idea into better processes, decisions, customer experiences and ways of working.

  1. Sometimes the answer is automation.
  2. Sometimes it is a new application.
  3. Sometimes it is better use of existing data.
  4. Sometimes the right answer is to do nothing yet.

Knowing the difference is our job.

Responsible AI

Use AI with confidence, not carelessness.

AI can improve how an organization works, but it can also introduce new risks. Those risks need to be understood before systems are put into everyday use. Nadovis Consult brings responsible AI considerations into the work from the beginning.

The aim is to make AI use deliberate without making it complicated.

  • Clear governance and accountability
  • Data and information considerations
  • Security and access controls
  • Human oversight
  • Clear internal usage expectations
  • Risk identification
  • Responsible deployment and adoption

What success looks like

AI should eventually show up in the numbers, the customer experience or the way people work.

  • Less time spent on repetitive work
  • Faster access to information
  • Better customer or citizen service
  • More consistent processes
  • Improved decision support
  • Lower operational friction
  • New products or services
  • Greater capacity without simply adding more work

We encourage clients to define the intended result before choosing the technology.
